Privacy Policy

Effective: July 22, 2026 | Updated: November 9, 2026

ForgeMate (the “App”) is operated by 戴明成. This policy explains what information we collect, how we obtain, use, and store it, and when we share it with third parties. Please read it before using the App.

ForgeMate offers generative AI features. Only after you give separate consent do we send the data listed on the consent screen to the Qwen model service on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio. Declining does not affect non-AI features such as workout logging, plan editing, and timers.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

  • Profile photo: You can optionally choose a photo from your library or take one. After you confirm, we upload it to private object storage to display your account avatar and sync it across devices. It is not sent to AI or used for advertising. We keep the processed avatar without the original photo's metadata, such as location. When you replace or remove the avatar or delete your account, we stop showing it and remove the associated file.
  • Account information: When you register or sign in, you provide a username and password. If you use WeChat sign-in in the WeChat Mini Program, WeChat returns an openid and may also return a unionid. We use this information to identify your account, keep you signed in, and sync data. Passwords are stored in a non-reversible form. We cannot read your plaintext password and do not receive your WeChat password.
  • Body and training information: During onboarding or in your profile, you can provide a nickname, avatar, sex, height, weight, body fat percentage, training experience, intensity, goals, target weight and body fat percentage, weekly training days, training location, and session duration. Information such as sex, height, weight, and body fat percentage is sensitive personal information. We ask for separate consent before you enter it. You can decline or skip this step. This may reduce personalization but does not affect basic training features.
  • Training plans and workout records: When you create, edit, or complete a workout, we collect exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, completion status, and workout records to display your plan, show progress, and provide workout reviews.
  • Chat content: When you send a message, we collect its text and the conversation history to maintain context, generate replies, and save your chats.
  • Voice input: When you use voice input and grant microphone and speech recognition permissions, your device converts speech to text. We receive only the resulting text and do not store or upload raw recordings to our servers.
  • Device and operational logs: When you use the service, our servers record necessary information such as request times, endpoint status, and errors for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. We do not use these logs for ad tracking.
  • Usage and interaction data: After you accept this policy and sign in, we automatically collect structured events at key steps such as registration, sign-in, onboarding, generating plans, starting or completing workouts, leaving workouts, and submitting feedback. Events may include the event name and time, account and app session IDs, platform and app version, workout date, exercise count, completion rate, and entry point. We use these events to understand feature usage, measure activation and retention, and improve reliability and the training experience. We do not collect these events when you are signed out. Events do not contain verbatim chats, full prompts, model responses, specific body measurements, contact details, or advertising IDs.

We do not collect information unrelated to providing these services or sell personal information.

2. Separate consent for generative AI

Recipient

The recipient is the Qwen model service on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio.

Data that may be sent

When you request an AI reply, plan, plan adjustment, or workout review, our servers send only the information needed for that request, selected from what you have already provided:

  • Your current message and recent chat history needed to maintain context.
  • Nickname, sex, height, weight, body fat percentage, and training experience.
  • Training goals and intensity, weekly training days, location, duration, target weight, and target body fat percentage.
  • Relevant existing training plans, workout records, or summaries.

We do not send your login username, password, WeChat openid or unionid, contact details, avatar, or raw voice recordings to Qwen.

Purpose and consent

The data is used only to understand your training needs and generate the requested reply, plan, adjustment, or review. Before your first AI message or plan generation, or before you use the relevant features again following a material policy change, the App shows a separate third-party AI data-sharing notice explaining the recipient, data categories, and purpose. We send the information only after you select “Agree and Send.” If you decline, no data is sent and non-AI features remain available.

You can withdraw this consent in your profile under consent management. We will stop sending data and ask for consent again if you later use these features.

Third-party safeguards

We require Alibaba Cloud Model Studio to process shared data only on our instructions and for the stated purposes, not for advertising or unrelated purposes, and to provide privacy and security safeguards at least equivalent to those described here. We review its safeguards in accordance with applicable laws and platform rules.

3. Other third-party processing

  • SMS sign-in and linking a phone number: If you choose this feature and accept this policy, we send your number and the code you submit to Alibaba Cloud Phone Number Verification Service (PNVS) to send and verify SMS codes. We retain the number's link to your account but do not store codes in plaintext or send marketing texts. Messages may use the preset sender name “恒创联众.” You can use your existing account or Apple sign-in without linking a number. We use verification requests, their purpose, timing, and outcomes to prevent replay and abuse. These records are periodically cleaned up, and associated phone identities and verification records are removed when you delete your account. Rate limiting uses a hashed identifier that does not directly expose the number.
  • System speech recognition: Speech-to-text uses operating system services, is controlled by system permissions, and is also subject to the operating system provider's privacy policy.
  • WeChat sign-in: If you use quick sign-in in the WeChat Mini Program, we send a one-time sign-in credential to WeChat to obtain an openid and, when available, a unionid. We do not receive your WeChat password or store the WeChat session_key.
  • Legal disclosures: We may disclose relevant information as required by law or as legally necessary to protect the rights and interests of users or the public.

4. Storage and retention

  • On your device: iOS sign-in credentials are stored in Keychain. Agreement and consent status and app settings are stored on your device. Separate consent is stored per account.
  • On our servers: Account data, body and training information, training plans, workout records, and chats are stored in Hangzhou, Zhejiang, mainland China, to provide the service and sync across devices.
  • Retention: While your account is active, we retain service data only as long as necessary for its purpose. When you delete your account, account data, body information, plans, workouts, chats, and other service data are deleted immediately. Usage and interaction data and operational logs are retained for up to 30 days and then deleted or anonymized. Mandatory legal retention requirements may apply.

5. Your rights

  • Access and correct: View and edit your information in your profile.
  • Withdraw consent: Use consent management in your profile to withdraw consent for generative AI or sensitive body information. If you withdraw the latter, we will, after your confirmation, clear your sex, height, weight, body fat percentage, target weight, and target body fat percentage from your device and our servers.
  • Delete data or your account: Request deletion in the App. Upon account deletion, service data is deleted immediately. Usage and interaction data and operational logs are retained for up to 30 days unless otherwise required by law.
  • Complaints and feedback: Use in-app feedback or email us at the address below.

Withdrawal does not affect processing based on consent before it was withdrawn.

6. Information security

We protect information using access controls, encryption in transit, non-reversible password storage, least-privilege access, and audit logs. If a security incident may affect your rights, we will provide timely notice and take remedial measures as required by law.

7. Minors

The App is primarily intended for adults. Minors should use it under a guardian's guidance and obtain their guardian's consent before providing personal information.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy. If the recipient, data categories, processing purpose, or other material details change, we will notify you in the App and ask for consent again before continuing to share data.
